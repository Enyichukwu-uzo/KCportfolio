01 About / Professional Identity

A scientist's precision. A writer's creativity. A teacher's clarity.

The disciplines may seem different on the surface — but they share one thread: the discipline of seeing clearly, thinking rigorously, and communicating with impact.

The intersection is the identity.

I am a Medical Laboratory Scientist by training — where every result must be precise, every sample handled with care, and every observation recorded without ambiguity. That analytical foundation shapes everything I do.

As an Optician, I translate that precision into patient care — understanding how people see, and helping them see better. As a Teacher, I break complex ideas into clear, learnable moments. As a Copywriter, I turn that clarity into stories that resonate. And as an AI Prompt Engineer, I bring all of it together — designing interactions between human intention and machine capability.
