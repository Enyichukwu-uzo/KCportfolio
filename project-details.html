03 Selected Work / Projects

Where disciplines converge in practice.

A closer look at the thinking, communication, and creative execution behind selected projects.

Copywriting

"Test Results, Decoded"

A 12-part patient-education series for a diagnostic laboratory in Port Harcourt.

What it is

A patient-education content series designed to help patients understand common laboratory investigations before discussing their results with a doctor.

Scientific Thinking

Reviewed laboratory SOPs and CLSI guidelines to ensure clinical accuracy across topics including CBC, LFT, HbA1c, and other common investigations.

Clear Communication

Complex laboratory information was translated into approximately a sixth-grade reading level while retaining the Nigerian context.

Creative Execution

Content followed a simple "What it means" and "Next steps" structure.

Developed the content for carousel posts, one-minute explainer reels, and SMS snippets.

Example title: "Your HbA1c is 7.2%. What does that mean for your eyes?"
Laboratory Science

"Zero Error Documentation"

A quality-assurance documentation framework for a clinical laboratory.

What it is

Designed and implemented a QA documentation system focused on improving consistency, traceability, and laboratory audit readiness.

Scientific Thinking

Mapped pre-analytical, analytical, and post-analytical errors using ISO 15189 standards. Control charts were used to monitor error rates.

Clear Communication

Created SOPs, checklists, and audit trails using plain language so that MLAs and interns could follow the processes without unnecessary confusion.

Creative Execution

Built a colour-coded QA Dashboard in Excel and created laminated workflow posters for use directly at the laboratory bench.

Impact: Reduced documentation errors by 52% in three months.
Opticianry

"See Better, Choose Better"

A visual patient-education guide for better lens and frame decisions.

What it is

Created a visual decision guide to help patients understand lens options, coatings, and frame selection.

Scientific Thinking

Explained refraction, pupillary distance, lens indices, UV protection, and other optical concepts using principles of optics.

Clear Communication

Replaced technical jargon with visual comparisons. For example, blue-light and night-driving lenses were presented side-by-side. "Good, Better, Best" lens packages were also used to make choices easier to compare.

Creative Execution

Designed A3 posters, WhatsApp infographics, and in-store tablet slides. Real patient before-and-after stories were used to make the information more relatable.

Result: 40% increase in AR coating uptake because patients understood the "why".
AI Prompt Engineering

"Literature to Manuscript in 3 Steps"

An AI-assisted research workflow for eye-health researchers.

What it is

Built a custom LLM workflow to help eye-health researchers draft systematic reviews and conference papers.

Scientific Thinking

Prompts were structured around IMRAD, PRISMA guidelines, and APA 7th referencing. A DOI validation step was also included.

Clear Communication

The workflow produces summaries, methods drafts, and discussion points in academic English before simplifying the material for lay summaries.

Creative Execution

Packaged the workflow as a Notion + ChatGPT template containing specialised tools such as a Reviewer Response Generator and Slide Deck from Paper prompts.

Workflow outcome: Two conference abstracts drafted in 48 hours instead of two weeks.
Teaching · Opticianry

"Frames to Formulas"

A six-week practical opticianry course combining theory, sales, and patient care.

What it is

A practical course for dispensing optician students designed to connect classroom theory with real patient-care situations.

Scientific Thinking

Lensometry, vertex distance, prism, and other technical concepts were taught through real case studies and physics formulas.

Clear Communication

Every technical topic ended with a practical communication exercise: "How would you explain this to a patient in 30 seconds?" Analogies and Pidgin were used where appropriate to make difficult concepts easier to understand.

Creative Execution

Created a Frame Styling Lab, Troubleshooting Clinic, role-play assessments, and a WhatsApp resource hub containing supporting videos.

Outcome: 90% pass rate on the first licensing examination.
Integrated

"The Precision-Expression Framework"

A personal methodology for approaching complex problems through science, communication, and creative execution.

What it is

A personal framework developed around the idea that complex knowledge becomes more useful when it can be understood, communicated, and applied effectively.

Scientific Thinking

Begin with evidence, established principles, structured analysis, and a clear understanding of the problem.

Clear Communication

Translate complexity according to the needs of the intended audience without sacrificing accuracy.

Creative Execution

Turn the resulting knowledge into something useful: written content, visual guides, teaching materials, digital workflows, AI systems, or practical tools.
